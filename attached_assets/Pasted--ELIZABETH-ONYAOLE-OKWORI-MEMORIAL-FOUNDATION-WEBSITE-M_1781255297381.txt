# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

The existing NGO training portal shall be upgraded and rebranded into a comprehensive Foundation website that combines:

* Free vocational training enrollment
* Foundation activities
* Sponsorship and partnership management
* Beneficiary fund disbursement requests
* Bakery and catering order management
* Educational resources and blog content
* Customer ordering and delivery tracking

The platform shall serve participants, customers, sponsors, partners, beneficiaries, and NGO officials through a unified system.
