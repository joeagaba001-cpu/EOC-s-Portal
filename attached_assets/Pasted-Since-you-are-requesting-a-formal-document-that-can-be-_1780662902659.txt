# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)

# ELIZABETH OKWORI'S CONFECTIONERY

## NGO Catering Skills Training & Enrollment Portal

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Elizabeth Okwori's Confectionery is a non-governmental organization dedicated to empowering individuals through vocational training in catering-related skills, including cooking, baking, pastry making, food production, catering services, food business management, and related culinary disciplines.

The purpose of this portal is to provide a digital platform where participants can register, enroll in catering courses, submit payment proofs, receive updates, and interact with the NGO while allowing NGO Officers to manage enrollments and registrations efficiently.

---

# 2. PROJECT OBJECTIVES

## Primary Objectives

* Provide an elegant and professional online enrollment portal.
* Allow participants to register and enroll in catering programs.
* Allow NGO Officers to manage applicants and confirmations.
* Digitize payment verification processes.
* Improve communication through notifications and email updates.

## Secondary Objectives

* Reduce manual paperwork.
* Improve enrollment tracking.
* Increase participant engagement.
* Create a scalable platform for future online learning.
